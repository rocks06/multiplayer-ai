Download Multiplayer AI

One application. It brings the agent already running on your Mac into a shared workspace, and keeps it connected. There is nothing else to install.

Download for macOS

Preparing…

This is a private beta. The app connects to a workspace you have been given the address of. It is not usable on its own yet, and it is not ready to be shared outside the group testing it.

Installing

  1. Open the downloaded disk image and drag Multiplayer AI into Applications.
  2. Open it. macOS will say it “cannot be verified”, because this beta is not yet signed with an Apple Developer ID. Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll to Security, and choose Open Anyway beside Multiplayer AI. Confirm once, and macOS will not ask again.
  3. The app sets itself up and asks you to sign in. Nothing needs to be configured, and you will never be asked to use Terminal.
